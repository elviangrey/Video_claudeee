**All the clips were recorded sideways** (landscape footage inside a 720×1280 portrait file with no
rotation flag). Each one was rotated 90° counter-clockwise to 1280×720, re-encoded to H.264 with a
keyframe every 15 frames so seeking is accurate, and saved to `assets/footage/`.

Event details were read off the poster in `Opening3.mp4`:
**Beyond the Pages: Library Week · Perpustakaan Tun Abdul Razak, Kampus Samarahan 2 · 2 – 5 PM**.
The poster has **no date**, so the teaser doesn't show one either.

## 2. Transcript

See `TRANSCRIPT.md`. Every clip was run through Whisper, and the loudness and silence of each
clip was measured with ffmpeg. The footage is B-roll with no dialogue: mean levels are −43 to
−52 dB, made up of room tone, footsteps and the odd page rustle. So the cuts are driven by
**picture and beat** rather than speech, all footage is muted, and the sound is carried by music
and SFX.

## 4. What was cut and why

- **Repetition:** the duplicate `5_…113.mp4`, the window moment in `Opening_2` (same idea as
  `Opening_1`), and the shelf pans at the start of `ending.mp4` (`Library ambience` covers the
  same idea better).
- **Awkward pauses and dead air:** the slow painting pan before the poster (`Opening3` 0–3.8 s),
  the static first 4.4 s of the peek shot, and the reach-in at the start of `Book transition`.
- **Hook:** the opening goes straight into the most mysterious image in the footage (old
  manuscripts) with a curiosity line, then answers it with a human moment (the peek and the
  "Psst"). The title drop lands on the music drop at 10.2 s.

## 5. Audio

All audio is original and generated by `tools/synth_audio.py` with numpy, no samples:
- `music_bed.wav`: A minor, Am(add9), Fmaj7, Cmaj7, G(add9). A soft heartbeat and music-box
  intro, a snare build, a drop at 10.2 s (kick, clap, hats, bass, pluck arpeggio), and an outro
  chord with bells at 30.6 s.
- SFX: whoosh, short whoosh, riser, impact, deep boom, pop, tick, shimmer, page flip, paper rustle.

## 6. Revision v2: a less "AI template" look

- Removed: floating dust particles (hook), all twinkling sparkles (title and end card),
  blur-in text, the bouncy overshoot easing, the "Psst" wiggle, and the pulsing CTA.
- Text now uses a simple fade and slide-up.
- SFX cut from 23 down to 7, keeping only the ones tied to a real action: a soft boom on the
  hook, a whoosh into the peek, the riser and impact for the title drop, a page flip on the wipe,
  a page flip on the flipping-pages shot, and a whoosh into the info scene. Removed: pops, ticks,
  shimmers, paper rustle, short whooshes, and the second impact on the end card. Volumes lowered.

## Alternate cut: CapCut vibes + Canva-style animation (`compositions/capcut-vibes.html`)

Same footage, music, beat grid and timing as v4 (35.64 s). `index.html` (v4) is unchanged.

- **CapCut-style effects:** punchy colour grade (contrast/saturation), zoom-blur transitions,
  camera shake on hits, white flash cuts, glitch bars + RGB split at the title, beat bounce on
  every beat (0.48 s) in the montage, whip-pan out of the title, a light leak at the info scene
  and the end card.
- **CapCut auto-caption look:** heavy white Montserrat with a black stroke, word-by-word pop, and
  the key word in a yellow sticker box (SECRET, YET?, STORY).
- **Canva-style text animations:** Typewriter (hook and end-card date), Pop ("Psst…" sticker,
  cards, CTA), Block reveal (title, logo), Stomp (EXPLORE / DISCOVER / GET LOST / UNCOVER, with
  outline echo rows), Rise (kicker, title lines), Breathe (CTA pulse).
- **SFX:** adds glitch, bass hit, swipe and shutter (`tools/synth_sfx_capcut.py`) on top of the
  existing set, 24 cues in total.

Render: `npx hyperframes render -c compositions/capcut-vibes.html -q delivery -o renders/beyond-the-pages-capcut.mp4`
